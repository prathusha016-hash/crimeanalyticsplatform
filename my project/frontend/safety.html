🛡 Personal Safety Tips

  • Stay alert in public places.
  • Avoid isolated areas at night.
  • Share live location when travelling late.
  • Keep emergency numbers saved.
  • Use trusted transport options.
  • Do not display valuables openly.
  • While travelling alone, carry a pepper spray for self-defense.

💻 Cyber Safety Tips

  • Never share OTP or passwords.
  • Use strong passwords.
  • Verify unknown links before clicking.
  • Report fraud immediately.

📌 If an Incident Happens

Move to a safe place, call emergency services, gather details, and report the matter immediately.
